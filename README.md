# Dream Weaver Capital

Build a polished, premium, trustworthy responsive website for an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor and Investment & Financial Services professional serving clients across India.

Business facts to use accurately:
- AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor
- ARN: 83625
- 15+ years of experience
- Serving 500+ families across India
- Core promise: helping families work toward their life dreams and financial goals comfortably through disciplined investing and goal-based planning.
- Do not invent SEBI registration, certifications, licenses, awards, AUM, returns, guarantees, office address, phone, email, or performance claims. Where contact details are missing, use clear placeholders that are easy to edit.
- Avoid any guaranteed-return language. Include an appropriate general investment-risk disclaimer and a note that mutual fund investments are subject to market risks.

Website goals:
1. Generate trust and qualified enquiries.
2. Clearly explain the distributor's approach: understand goals → assess risk/time horizon → recommend suitable mutual fund solutions → monitor/review periodically.
3. Make the site feel personal and relationship-led, not like a generic fintech portal.
4. Strong mobile experience.

Pages/sections:
- Home: elegant hero with headline such as “Invest with Purpose. Plan for the Life You Want.” and supporting copy highlighting 15+ years, 500+ families, India-wide service, and ARN 83625. Primary CTA “Book a Consultation”; secondary CTA “Explore Our Services”.
- About: professional story and values, using only facts supplied above.
- Services: Goal-based investment planning, mutual fund distribution, SIP planning, retirement/financial goal planning, portfolio review/rebalancing guidance, and family wealth planning. Phrase these as services offered and avoid implying regulated advisory services unless appropriately qualified.
- Process: 4 simple steps (Discover, Plan, Invest, Review).
- Why Families Choose Us: relationship, disciplined approach, goal focus, long-term support, transparent communication.
- Goal sections/cards: Retirement, Child Education, Wealth Creation, Home/Other Life Goals, Emergency/Financial Resilience.
- Educational section: simple explanations of SIP, mutual funds, risk profiling, asset allocation, compounding, and long-term investing. Include no personalized investment advice.
- FAQ.
- Contact/Book Consultation form with name, phone, email, city, goal, investment horizon, approximate investment amount (optional), and message. Include consent checkbox.
- Footer with ARN 83625, risk disclaimer, privacy/terms placeholders.

Design:
- Premium Indian financial-services aesthetic: deep navy/charcoal with warm gold accents, lots of white space, sophisticated typography, subtle gradients, tasteful financial/Indian family imagery, restrained animations.
- Avoid stock-market clichés, excessive charts, neon colors, crypto imagery, and aggressive sales language.
- Use accessible contrast, responsive cards, sticky mobile CTA, clear navigation.
- Add tasteful trust-stat counters: “15+ Years”, “500+ Families”, “Across India”, “ARN 83625”.
- Include a WhatsApp/contact CTA but use a placeholder number until provided.
- SEO-ready page titles/meta descriptions and semantic headings. Use a professional brand name placeholder like “[Your Name] | Mutual Fund Distributor” where the actual name has not been provided.
- Build the complete website now with clean reusable components and realistic copy based strictly on the information above. Make it easy to replace placeholders later.
